# Battle 1 · Dead Channel

The relay has taken over Mr. L’s voice and movement. His hollow eyes and stretched silhouette take their cues from [Overdue (2)](https://marios-madness.fandom.com/wiki/Mr._L/Gallery). The violet fractures show the relay broadcasting through him. Boyfriend follows the rhythm to locate the source. The signal cuts out after the final response, leaving its source for the next battle.

## Patterns

The chart uses six call-and-response pairs at 120 BPM. Follow the four displayed arrows throughout. During the mirrored pairs, the chart swaps left with right and down with up in the player's answer.

| Song time | Pattern |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 seconds | Count-in |
| 2–10 seconds | Two echo pairs |
| 10–18 seconds | Two mirrored pairs, each introduced by a two-tone cue |
| 18–26 seconds | Two echo pairs with short holds |
| 26–28 seconds | Outro |

Six gold bullet notes appear at 4.5, 8.5, 13, 16.5, 20.5, and 24.5 seconds. Hit them like regular notes. The bottom-left tracker starts with three filled bullets and removes one per bullet miss. Hits do not refill the tracker. The third bullet miss triggers game over. A retry restores all three chances. Both Boyfriend and Pico use the same chart.

## Parent playtest

- Help the player find **Dead Channel** in Freeplay with each character and listen for its preview.
- Watch Mr. L lean back left, crouch low with both arms out, stretch up, and throw an L gesture to the right during his call. Check that his boots stay visible and that he returns to his idle pose.
- Ask the player what changes after the two-tone cue. Hold the final sustained notes until their tails clear.
- Miss a gold bullet and then hit the next one. Check that the tracker stays at two chances.
- Miss three gold bullets total while hitting the regular notes. Check that the third miss triggers game over and retry restores all three icons.
- If anything fails, record the symptom and the step where it happened. Crop screenshots to the game window before sharing.

If the song is missing, confirm the game version, that the mod is enabled, and that the metadata file is directly inside the copied mod folder. Restart the game after changing files. To uninstall the demo, close the game and remove its copied folder from `mods`.
